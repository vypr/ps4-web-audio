Listen to your PS4 anywhere on your LAN

Made by vypr · github.com/vypr

PS4 → local network → PC/phone browser → speakers or Bluetooth headphones. Every listener chooses their own audio profile. Joining or leaving should not interrupt the other listeners.

Choose your audio quality

Fastest favors latency over fidelity and buffering. Highest quality preserves source precision, but depends more on LAN bandwidth and adds playback delay. Actual delay depends on your browser, network and Bluetooth headset.
